Plot Synopsis :

Interstellar follows a dying Earth in the near future where a global crop blight and severe dust storms threaten humanity with extinction. Joseph Cooper, a widowed former NASA pilot turned farmer, discovers a strange gravitational anomaly in his daughter Murph's bedroom that reveals geographic coordinates. These coordinates lead them to a secret, underground NASA facility led by Professor Brand.

Brand reveals that a mysterious wormhole, likely created by an advanced intelligence, has opened near Saturn, offering a pathway to another galaxy with three potentially habitable planets. Cooper is recruited to pilot the Endurance spacecraft alongside a team of scientists, including Brand’s daughter Amelia, leaving his heartbroken children behind on a desperate mission to find a new home for mankind.
